# Salary Bands 2026 (HR CONFIDENTIAL)

This document is restricted to the HR team and executives. Do not share with other employees.

## Engineering
| Level | Title | Annual salary range (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Junior Engineer | 70,000 - 90,000 |
| E2 | Software Engineer | 90,000 - 120,000 |
| E3 | Senior Engineer | 120,000 - 155,000 |
| E4 | Staff Engineer | 155,000 - 195,000 |

## Finance and Operations
| Level | Title | Annual salary range (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Financial Analyst | 65,000 - 85,000 |
| F2 | Senior Analyst | 85,000 - 110,000 |
| F3 | Finance Manager | 110,000 - 140,000 |

## Bonus structure
- Individual bonus target: 8% of base salary for levels E1-E2 and F1-F2, 12% for E3 and above and F3
- Company performance multiplier ranges from 0.5x to 1.5x
- Bonuses are paid in March for the previous calendar year

## Salary review cycle
Reviews take place each April. The 2026 merit budget is 4% of total payroll.
