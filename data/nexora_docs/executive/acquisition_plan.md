# Project Harbor: Acquisition Plan (EXECUTIVE ONLY, STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL)

## Purpose
Nexora is evaluating the acquisition of RouteWise, a 35-person startup that makes route optimization software, to strengthen NexoRoute.

## Key terms under discussion
- Indicative price: 14 to 18 million USD (cash and shares)
- Expected closing: Q1 2027
- Key retention: the 6 senior engineers at RouteWise would receive 2-year retention packages

## Rationale
- Cuts the NexoRoute roadmap by an estimated 9 months
- Adds 85 customers in Europe
- Estimated annual cost savings of 1.2 million USD after integration

## Risks
- Integration of two engineering cultures
- Dependence on one RouteWise founder who plans to leave after 12 months

## Disclosure rules
This plan must not be shared with any employee outside the executive team, the Board and the external legal advisers until the announcement date.
