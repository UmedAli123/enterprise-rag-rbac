# Code of Conduct

All Nexora employees, contractors and interns are expected to follow this code.

## Respect and inclusion
We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination or bullying of any kind. This applies in the office, online, and at company events.

## Confidentiality
Customer data, source code, financial results and personnel information are confidential. Do not share them outside the company or with colleagues who do not need them for their work.

## Conflicts of interest
Disclose any outside business interest, board role or financial stake in a supplier, customer or competitor to your manager and to HR.

## Gifts and hospitality
Gifts from suppliers or customers worth more than 75 USD must be declined or reported to your manager.

## Reporting concerns
Raise concerns with your manager, HR, or the anonymous Ethics Line at ethics@nexora-demo.example. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is not tolerated and may lead to dismissal.
