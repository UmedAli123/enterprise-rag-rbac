# Nexora Technologies: Company Overview

Nexora Technologies is a mid-sized software company founded in 2018 and headquartered in Austin, Texas, with remote teams in Toronto and Lisbon. We build cloud-based inventory and logistics software for retail and warehouse businesses.

## Mission
To help businesses move goods faster with fewer errors through reliable, easy-to-use software.

## Company facts
- Employees: about 240 across 5 departments
- Products: NexoStock (inventory management), NexoRoute (delivery routing), NexoInsight (analytics dashboard)
- Customers: more than 600 retail and logistics clients in North America and Europe
- Core values: Customer first, Own the outcome, Keep it simple, Share knowledge

## Departments
Engineering, Product, Sales and Marketing, Finance, and People Operations (HR).

## Working hours
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in each employee's local time zone. Teams may agree their own schedules outside these hours.

- Toronto: 88 Queen Street East, Floor 4
