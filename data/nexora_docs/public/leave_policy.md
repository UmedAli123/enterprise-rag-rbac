# Leave and Time-Off Policy

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Nexora Technologies.

## Annual leave
- 0 to 2 years of service: 18 days per year
- 3 to 5 years of service: 22 days per year
- 6 or more years of service: 26 days per year
- Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next calendar year.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A medical note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
- Primary caregivers: 16 weeks of paid leave
- Secondary caregivers: 6 weeks of paid leave
- Leave can be taken any time within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Public holidays
Employees receive the public holidays of the country where they are based. Austin staff also receive the day after Thanksgiving and December 24.

## How to request leave
Submit the request in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for leave longer than 3 days. Your manager will approve or decline within 3 working days.
