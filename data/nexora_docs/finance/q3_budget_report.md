# Q3 2026 Budget Report (FINANCE CONFIDENTIAL)

## Summary
| Item | Budget (USD) | Actual (USD) | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 12,500,000 | 12,940,000 | +3.5% |
| Cost of goods sold | 3,100,000 | 3,020,000 | -2.6% |
| Engineering spend | 4,200,000 | 4,410,000 | +5.0% |
| Sales and marketing | 2,300,000 | 2,150,000 | -6.5% |
| General and administrative | 1,100,000 | 1,085,000 | -1.4% |
| Operating profit | 1,800,000 | 2,275,000 | +26.4% |

## Highlights
- Revenue beat the budget mainly because of 42 new NexoRoute customers in North America.
- Engineering overspend was caused by higher cloud hosting costs after the August traffic peak (see incident report ENG-002).
- Sales and marketing underspent because two planned trade shows were moved to Q4.

## Q4 outlook
Forecast revenue is 13.4 million USD. Planned hiring is 12 roles, mostly in Engineering. Cloud cost reduction target is 8% through reserved instances.
